One thing worth flagging that I found but did not act on: `risk.risk_per_trade_pct` is effectively **inert**. `backtest.py:153` sizes each position at `min(equity·1%/ATR, equity·20%/price, cash/price)`, and for a typical 2% ATR stock the risk term works out to ~50% of equity — so the 20% notional cap always binds. Position sizing is equal-dollar, not risk-parity, which is why the high-ATR names (INTC, AMD, AVGO) dominate both the worst-10 list and the biggest wins. That's a structural lever for a future round, but changing it would rescale every trade at once and I did not want to spend this round's single change on something that unpredictable.
