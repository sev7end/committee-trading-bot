**Change made:** `trend.weight` 1.0 → 2.0 in `config/params.json` (one weight, nothing else).

**Why this and not something else:**

- In plain terms: the committee currently ranks "the stock just poked above its 20-day high" as more important than "the stock is genuinely in an uptrend." The trade log says that's backwards. Every trade where the trend voter was *neutral* — price and its two moving averages disagreeing, so the stock was chopping or rallying inside a downtrend — averaged a small loss, and those 12 trades in the first half account for the entire first-half deficit that keeps failing the robustness check.
- The mechanism matters more than the threshold. Because only 5 positions can be open and 952 signals were turned away for lack of a slot, the weights mostly decide *which* candidates get picked, not how many. With trend at 2.0, a clean uptrend with no breakout (score 5.0 + volume) now outranks a breakout in a non-trending stock (4.5 + volume) — an inversion of today's ordering, and the two buckets earn +$77/trade and -$5/trade respectively.
- I deliberately avoided the tempting alternatives: widening the 1-ATR stop would have been a leverage increase in disguise (position size is pinned by the 20%-of-equity cap, not by `risk_per_trade_pct`, so a wider stop makes each loss bigger rather than each position smaller), and an RSI "don't chase" voter would have vetoed the April–May winners (INTC, AMD, QCOM) that carried the window.

Hypothesis written to `results/rounds/round_2.hypothesis.txt`. I did not run the backtest — the loop's acceptance check will score it.
